What ShadowPlayer does

ShadowPlayer plays videos from your own photo library and lets you loop any section over and over — designed for shadowing practice, language study, and drilling a passage until it sticks.

  • A-B repeat — mark a start and end point; playback loops between them indefinitely
  • Word lists — jot down words per video while you practice
  • Word meanings — show a meaning beneath each word, translated on your device
  • Playlists — group videos, and view every video's words merged into one list
  • Playback speed — 0.8× to 1.2× with natural voice pitch, remembered per video
  • Resume — picks up where you left off
  • Background audio — keeps playing when the screen is locked

Frequently asked questions

How do I set an A-B loop?

While playing, tap Set A at the point you want the loop to start, then Set B where it should end. Playback then repeats that section until you tap Cancel.

Why does the app need photo library access?

To let you pick which videos to play. ShadowPlayer only reads the videos you select — it never adds, modifies, or deletes anything in your library, and nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

Where are my videos stored?

They stay in your photo library. ShadowPlayer plays them directly and never makes copies, so it uses almost no extra storage.

A video disappeared from my list

ShadowPlayer references videos in your photo library. If a video is deleted from the library, it can no longer be played.

Can I play videos in the background?

Yes. Audio keeps playing when you lock the screen or return to the home screen — useful for listening practice.

How do I fast-forward precisely?

Tap the forward/back buttons to jump 3 seconds. Press and hold to scan continuously, and release to stop.

ShadowPlayer 是什么

ShadowPlayer 播放你相册里的视频，并可以把任意片段反复循环——专为影子跟读、语言学习和反复练习设计。

  • A-B 循环——标记起点和终点，在两点之间无限重复
  • 单词表——练习时随手记录每个视频的生词
  • 单词释义——在单词下方显示释义，翻译在你的设备上完成
  • 播放列表——把视频归类，并可查看整个列表所有视频的合并单词表
  • 倍速播放——0.8× 至 1.2×，音调保持自然，并按视频分别记住
  • 断点续播——从上次结束的位置继续
  • 后台播放——锁屏后声音继续播放

常见问题

如何设置 A-B 循环？

播放时，在想要循环开始的位置点 Set A，在结束位置点 Set B，之后就会在这一段之间反复播放，点 Cancel 取消。

为什么需要相册权限？

用于让你选择要播放的视频。ShadowPlayer 只读取你选中的视频，从不添加、修改或删除相册内容，也不会上传任何数据。详见隐私政策。

视频保存在哪里？

视频始终留在你的相册中。ShadowPlayer 直接播放，不会产生副本，因此几乎不占用额外存储空间。

列表里的视频不见了

ShadowPlayer 引用的是相册中的视频。如果视频已从相册中删除，就无法再播放。

可以后台播放吗？

可以。锁屏或回到主屏幕后声音会继续播放，适合听力练习。

如何精确快进？

点击前进/后退按钮跳转 3 秒；长按可连续快进/快退，松手停止。
