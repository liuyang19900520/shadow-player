ShadowPlayer

Loop any part of a video, as many times as it takes.

A minimalist iOS video player built for shadowing practice and language study. Play videos from your own photo library, mark a section, and let it repeat while you practice.

Features

  • A-B repeatMark a start and end point — the section loops indefinitely until you cancel.
  • Word listsJot down words per video while you practice, and review them later.
  • PlaylistsGroup videos together and see every video's words merged into one editable list.
  • Playback speed0.8× to 1.2×, with the voice pitch kept natural.
  • Precise seekingTap to jump 3 seconds, or press and hold to scan.
  • Resume & background audioPicks up where you left off, and keeps playing when the screen is locked.
  • Completely privateNo account, no network requests, no data collection. Everything stays on your device.
