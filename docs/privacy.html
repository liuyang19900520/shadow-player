Privacy Policy

ShadowPlayer · Last updated: August 27, 2026

ShadowPlayer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

ShadowPlayer has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. The app makes no network requests of any kind. Everything you create stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your iPhone only, and is removed when you delete the app:

  • Your list of recently played videos (references only — the videos themselves are never copied)
  • Playlists you create
  • Word lists you write
  • Playback position for each video

None of this is uploaded anywhere, and it is not accessible to us or to any third party.

Photo library access

ShadowPlayer asks for permission to access your photo library so you can choose videos to play. This access is used only to read and play the videos you personally select.

  • The app never adds to, modifies, or deletes anything in your photo library
  • Videos are played directly from your library — no copies are made
  • No photo or video ever leaves your device

You may revoke this permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › ShadowPlayer.

Third parties

ShadowPlayer contains no third-party SDKs, trackers, or analytics libraries. No data is shared with anyone.

Children's privacy

Because ShadowPlayer collects no data at all, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.

隐私政策

ShadowPlayer · 最后更新：2026 年 8 月 27 日

ShadowPlayer 不收集、不传输、不共享任何个人数据。

本 App 没有服务器、没有账号、没有数据统计、没有广告，也不发起任何网络请求。 你创建的所有内容都只保存在你自己的设备上。

保存在你设备上的数据

以下内容仅保存在你的 iPhone 本地，卸载 App 后即被删除：

  • 最近播放的视频记录（仅保存引用，从不复制视频本身）
  • 你创建的播放列表
  • 你记录的单词表
  • 每个视频的播放进度

这些数据不会上传到任何地方，我们和任何第三方都无法访问。

关于相册权限

ShadowPlayer 请求相册权限，是为了让你能选择要播放的视频。该权限仅用于读取和播放你本人选择的视频。

  • App 从不添加、修改或删除你相册中的任何内容
  • 视频直接从相册流式播放，不会产生副本
  • 任何照片或视频都不会离开你的设备

你可以随时在 设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 照片 › ShadowPlayer 中撤销该权限。

第三方

本 App 不包含任何第三方 SDK、追踪器或数据统计库，不与任何人共享数据。

儿童隐私

由于本 App 完全不收集数据，因此也不会收集儿童的任何数据。
