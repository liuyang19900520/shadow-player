Shake to add a line
Loop a line three times and Kage Loop offers to add it to your word list: shake your iPhone, or tap Add. What was said is recognised on your device, and the audio never leaves your iPhone. Requires iOS 26 on a supported iPhone.

Choose the audio language once per playlist
Under Languages, pick the language spoken in a playlist's videos. Adding looped lines needs it.

Long words and meanings show in full
Long phrases and their meanings now wrap onto as many lines as they need, instead of being cut off.

Meanings follow your edits
Change a word, tap Done, and its meaning is translated again.
