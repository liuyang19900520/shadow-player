Kage Loop plays videos from the user's own photo library. No account or login is required.

To test the core features:
1. Launch the app, tap "+" at the top right of Home, choose to add a video from Photos, allow photo library access, and pick any video in which someone speaks. Assign it to a playlist.
2. In the player, tap "Set A", let it play a few seconds, then tap "Set B". The section repeats.
3. In the word list, turn on "Show meanings", tap "Add word", type a word and tap Done. A meaning appears beneath it.

New in 1.4, daily close-reading check-in:
4. In the word list, tap "Languages" and set the playlist's audio language to English or Japanese.
5. Let an A-B section repeat three times (between 5:00 and 23:00 local time). Return to Home: the Today card shows the section's length added to that language's minutes (goal: 15 minutes per language per day).
6. Tap the Today card to open the monthly calendar, and tap a day to see its minutes and new words.

Check-in data is stored only on the device. It is not synced, and the app makes no network requests of its own. There are no notifications and no account.

The shake-to-add feature from 1.3 is unchanged: it uses Apple's on-device speech recognition (iOS 26) and is hidden on devices that do not support it. Word meanings use Apple's on-device Translation framework (iOS 18 and later).

The photo library permission is used only to read videos the user selects. The app never adds to, modifies, or deletes anything in the library.

Note: the review device needs at least one video with speech in its photo library.
