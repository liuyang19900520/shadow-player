Daily close-reading check-in
Set a goal of 15 minutes of video a day for English and for Japanese. A section counts once you loop it three times between 5:00 and 23:00, using the playlist's audio language. Overlapping sections on the same day count once. Your progress is kept on your iPhone only.

A new Home screen
Home now opens with today's progress and this week, followed by Continue to resume the last video where you stopped. Tap today's card for a monthly calendar and each day's minutes and new words.

Add videos with +
